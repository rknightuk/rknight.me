When I posted about [my zine](https://rknight.me/blog/types-of-time-travel-zine/) I mentioned being surprised that there wasn't any tools for showing zines online like a web component or similar. Turns out there is, web search is just shit now.

[Andy](https://macaw.social/@andypiper/117062602771968355) pointed me to [Zine Machine](https://rowan.fyi/made/zine-machine//) which is a web-based viewer that uses print styles to switch between web and print layouts. Benji is doing something [similar with their zine here](https://www.benji.dog/the-trail-of-political-consciousness). 

Then there's [Dirty Little Zine](https://dirtylittlezine.com/) which is a web based zine maker.

I added some notes to my list about zine-related projects I wanted to try and one of them was a zine splitter. The idea was to take an image and split it into eight separate images for viewing online, or downloading. 

[Ragman beat me to it](https://jawns.club/@ragman/117090210182789703). He built [Zine Cutter](https://ragman.net/zinecutter/) which works perfectly and has saved me a job.
