I announced the [shut down EchoFeed](https://rknight.me/blog/shutting-down-echofeed/) which I intend to write more about soon. I shipped [Doodle Scan](https://doodlescan.rknight.me) this week which you can [read about here](https://rknight.me/blog/doodle-scan/). I also [made a zine](https://rknight.me/blog/types-of-time-travel-zine/) and I was [on a podcast](https://rknight.me/blog/i-was-a-guest-on-web-weaving/). I have a couple of other things I'm working on which should be out soon.

I've finally had time to sort through an obscene amount of links I had saved and there was a lot of good stuff in there. Who knew?

Chris [built his own font](https://chriskirknielsen.com/blog/lessons-learned-making-my-peritel-typeface/) and has lots of tips about how to go about doing that.

[delphitools](https://delphi.tools/) is a collection of "_privacy-first browser tools_" which is what I was going for with [DoodleScan](https://doodlescan.rknight.me) and some other upcoming projects. Lots of good stuff here.

[Matt & Nick's Stupid Adventure](https://stupidadventure.netlify.app/#) is a review blog ranking energy drinks. No RSS feed though, boo 👎.

A couple of little website dinguses - [Pocket Bird](https://idreesinc.itch.io/pocket-bird) and [TownSquare](https://townsquare.cauenapier.com/). Fun.

[The Closet](https://thecriterioncloset.com/#closet) allows you to browse all the Criterion movies as if you're in the closet. I have bad movie opinions so there's nothing here for me.

A script to make detect similar images in your photo library and make [wigglegrams](https://lmao.center/blog/wiggle-accidents/) from them. [What's a Wigglegram?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiggle_stereoscopy).

[CSS Buttons](https://cssbuttons.io/) is a collection of nice buttons.

[Improved CSS Text-Stroke](https://tylersticka.com/journal/improved-css-text-stroke/) and [text stickers](https://codepen.io/vii120/pen/vEggVwV). Wild what you can do with CSS now.

[The Definitive Guide to Neubrutalist Web Design](https://neubrutalism.com/). Hot.

[The Website Specification](https://specification.website/) is a useful reference for all the things you need to include in your website like metadata and such.

I love these [doodle projects](https://fountainpenlove.com/tag/a3-doodle/) from Fountain Pen Love.

Do I need another hobby? No. Do I want to [make my own rubber stamps? Yes.](https://josenaranja.blogspot.com/2026/07/homemade-rubber-stamps.html?m=1)

[Noice - Noice helps generate SVG filters for noise textures.](https://noice.vercel.app/)

[chiriapp/chiri: 🍃 Cross-platform CalDAV task management app.](https://github.com/chiriapp/chiri)

Computer Modern is the font made for TeX. [This project](https://checkmyworking.com/cm-web-fonts/) converts them all to web-friendly formats.

[A bookmarklet to quickly check how your alt texts are doing.](https://github.com/Hamatti/alt-text-debugging-bookmarklet). Nice.

This [Proof of Purchase](https://katebingamanburt.substack.com/p/proof-of-purchase-daily-purchase) project is so cool.

Another editor called [GRAM](https://gram-editor.com/). It has a frog logo so worth checking out.
