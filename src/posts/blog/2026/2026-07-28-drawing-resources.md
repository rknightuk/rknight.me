I asked for [recommendations for courses and books](https://rknight.me/notes/202607241729/) related to learning drawing techniques and I got a bunch of great suggestions. I've not tried any of these yet (except a single video from [Alphonso Dunn](https://www.youtube.com/@alphonsodunn) but I want to collect these in one place I can refer back to.

- [Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain](https://www.drawright.com) by Betty Edwards via [Kerri](https://social.lol/@digitalsnow/116976188532171005) and [Valrus](https://icosahedron.website/@valrus/116976758255735047). There's a few editions of this but I'll pick up a second hand copy of whichever one I come across first.
- Books by [Christopher Hart](https://christopherhartbooks.com/mybooks/) via [Andy](https://social.lol/@andycarolan/116976207039992957) and [alienlebarge](https://social.lol/@alienlebarge/116976231988180466). I have one of these on the way to check it out.
- The aforementioned Alphonso Dunn has a book called Pen and Ink Drawing, A Simple Guide. I found a copy of this really cheap on Vinted which is on its way to me. He also has a huge number of videos on his YouTube channel. via [Hollie](https://social.coop/@hollie/116976207352426536) and [kalikambo](https://social.lol/@kalikambo/116984724864720179)
- Maggie Appleton has a [great list of courses and books](https://maggieappleton.com/illustration-resources) via [Eric](https://front-end.social/@eeeps/116978912177621712)
- Eric also suggested [Fun with a Pencil](https://archive.org/details/andrew-loomis-fun-with-a-pencil) and [The Natural Way to Draw](https://ia601901.us.archive.org/19/items/The_Natural_Way_To_Draw_by_Kimon_Nicolaides/The_Natural_Way_To_Draw_by_Kimon_Nicolaides.pdf)
- [Draw](https://www.abebooks.co.uk/9780863180392/Draw-Master-Art-Jeffery-Camp-0863180396/plp) by Jeffrey Camp and [Drawing on the Artist Within](https://www.drawright.com/bettys-books), another Betty Edwards one came via [Gary](https://social.lol/@gary/116976523217332774).

I'm not going to get everything on this list because that would be chaos but there's lots of great stuff in here that I can choose from.
